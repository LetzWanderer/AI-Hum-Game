Build the game

After editing files in content/, use this page to make a fresh START-HERE.html. Everything happens on your computer — nothing is uploaded.

  1. Click the button and choose the whole game folder (ai-humanitarian-game). Your browser may ask to confirm — that is fine.
  2. Your content is checked first. Fix any red items and try again.
  3. Click Download START-HERE.html and save it into the game folder, replacing the old one.
